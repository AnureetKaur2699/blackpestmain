About Black's Pest Services

About Us

After working several years as a territory manager for a national pest control company, Bernard Black decided that he was ready to start his own small pest control business. In the summer of 2005, Black's Pest Services was born.


He recognized that his northwest customers were sensitive to changes in the pest control industry and had more questions about his products. To meet their needs, Bernard adopted the concept of bio-rational pest control into his business, also referred to as integrated pest management. This concept does not eliminate the use of pesticides but rather employs them in a way that makes them more efficient. In appropriate situations, this can reduce or even eliminate pesticide treatment for a particular job.

Urgency Reinforcement: Pest problems rarely go away on their own — they often get worse over time.
