About the Owner

Bernard always had a strong interest in insects. As a boy, he would run around collecting bugs to identify and keep as pets. When he grew older, he decided to pursue his interests further by attending UC Berkeley for his degree in Pest Management. Shortly after graduation, Bernard took a job with a local pest control business. He quickly realized that he was an entrepreneur at heart and decided to start his first company in Kingsburg California called Banner Pest Control.


In 1999, he sold Banner Pest Control to a national company and took a pest management position in Portland Oregon to be closer to his family. Once again he found his entrepreneurial spirit calling and decided to start Black's Pest Services.


Bernard has a strong background in both commercial and residential settings. His recent achievements include serving as an expert witness and becoming a Board Certified Entomologist. When Bernard is not working on the business or giving presentations and classes, he spends his time with his family or indulging in one of his favorite hobbies: gardening and beekeeping.

We Cover 100+ Different Species

*Not including wild animals e.g. birds, raccoons, skunks, etc.

Odorous House Ants

The most common house-invading ant in Oregon, attracted to sweets and moisture, leaving a distinct coconut-like smell when crushed.

Rodents

Mice and rats that enter homes during cold, wet Pacific Northwest months, contaminating surfaces and damaging insulation and wiring.

Spiders

Includes common home invaders like giant house spiders and cellar spiders, along with venomous species such as black widows and hobo spiders.

Stinging Insects

Aggressive summertime pests—including yellowjackets, paper wasps, mud daubers, and hornets—that build nests around eaves, decks, and underground.

Carpenter Ants

A major structural threat in Oregon that excavates galleries inside damp or sound wood to build massive colonies.

Earwigs

Moisture-seeking insects that enter lower floors, basements, and bathrooms during hot or overly wet weather.

Silverfish

Fast-moving nocturnal pests that thrive in high humidity and feed on wallpaper glue, cardboard, book bindings, and fabrics.

Stink Bugs and Boxelder Bugs

Nuisance bugs that gather on warm siding in late summer and attempt to overwinter inside wall voids and attics.

Bed Bugs

Parasitic insects that hide in mattress seams, bed frames, and baseboards, causing itchy bites and requiring specialized treatments.

Termites

Subterranean and dampwood termites that quietly feed on structural timbers, compromising a home's integrity over time.
